# Final readiness assessment

**The measurement record is sufficient for a scoped MSc hardware characterization of Xeon Max 9480 on the measured CRESCO8 hosts.** The chapter can report Flat and Cache behavior, bandwidth and latency under load, locality, native throughput, projection regimes and scoped energy. The [summary](FINAL_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md) and [evidence matrix](SCIENTIFIC_CLAIMS.md) define the defensible wording.

- **Ready.** Native per-cycle and frequency-qualified rates, local and concurrent bandwidth, dependent-load latency, the remote-access penalty, Cache working-set sensitivity, and the Flat BF16 and INT8 projection Rooflines. The [curated outputs](THESIS_FIGURES_AND_TABLES.md) link measured values to regenerated figures, tables and campaign IDs.
- **Corrected.** The Cache atlas now compares same-day lookup-byte evidence at matched working sets. The Roofline audit keeps raw crossings and the descriptive CV threshold. The principal figures use precision-specific compute references. Claims distinguish modeled tensor bytes from CAS or Cache lookup rates, and projection benchmarks from full-model scenarios.
- **Qualified.** PCU readings establish active power limiting in the recorded 350 W regime. Core count, transient windows and data-pattern controls support a relation between uncore clock and HBM bandwidth. They leave the counterfactual higher-power rate, component-level power allocation and the exact resource bottleneck unresolved. Cache repeatability is local to the measured state and time. The source of the excess remote-HBM transactions is unknown.
- **Reproducibility.** The October corpus is selected by `configs/analysis.json`, with raw records, exclusions, warnings and failed attempts retained. November read-search results live under a separate manifest. The archived records reproduce the search tables, while the exact November runner revision has a recorded source-hash mismatch with the current runner.
- **Verification.** A clean local build from raw records executed all eight notebooks and produced 41 SVG/PNG pairs, including the six principal figures. The test suite passed all 245 tests. The primary validator reported zero errors and retained 122 warnings. The Roofline ledger records 602 comparisons, including 164 matched counted GEMMs with zero crossings. Historical modeled and FP64 comparisons retain 19 raw crossings, of which 11 exceed the descriptive CV threshold.
- **Experiments.** The [final gap plan](EXPERIMENTAL_GAP_PLAN.md) identifies no blocking cluster run. A causal PL1 intervention or real-weight pattern test would expand the scope. Full-model tokens/s, inference energy and tensor-parallel scaling belong to the inference evaluation.

Build and validation commands are in the [repository guide](../README.md). The atlas and archived experiments remain available for defense questions and alternative reference choices.
