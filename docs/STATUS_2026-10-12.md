### First counted-against-modeled results (2026-10-12, `counted`)

Preliminary: computed from the fetched campaigns, not yet part of the committed analysis.
Counted bytes are the cores' DRAM line reads plus writes; `counted/modeled` of 1 means the
model describes the traffic.

| Workload | Flat DDR | Flat HBM | Cache 8 GiB | Cache 96 GiB / 16 GiB pool |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| read-only, 8 GiB, 56 cores | 0.992 | 0.992 | 0.992 | 1.000 |
| Triad | 0.991 | 0.990 | 0.991 | 1.003 |
| non-temporal Triad | 0.990 | 0.989 | 0.990 | 1.000 |
| FP64 sweep, K = 1 / 16 / 256 | 0.990 / 0.991 / 0.990 | 0.990 / 0.990 / 0.990 | 0.991 / 0.991 / 0.990 | 1.000 |
| BF16 14336×4096, M = 1 / 16 / 256 | 0.91 / 0.91 / 1.37 | 0.91 / 0.91 / 1.41 | n/a | 0.999 / 0.994 / 1.41 |
| INT8 14336×4096, M = 1 / 16 / 256 | 0.90 / 0.89 / 0.82 | 0.90 / 0.89 / 0.81 | n/a | 0.997 / 0.985 / 0.83 |

What they say:

* **The stream and sweep models are right to 1%.** Triad's read share is 0.664 (two of three
  arrays), the sweep's 0.495: no read-for-ownership at 56 cores on any tier. The
  write-allocate inference is now a measurement.
* **A5 is confirmed.** The Flat GEMM weight pool (about 1 GB) is partly served on chip: at
  small M only 90–91% of the modeled weight bytes reach DRAM. Counted bandwidth is then
  below the DDR ceiling (for example 281 GB/s modeled × 0.895 ≈ 251 GB/s against 262),
  which explains most of the DDR GEMM points above their ceiling (A4). The Cache runs with
  a 16 GiB pool count 0.99–1.00.
* **The HBM sweep excess stays.** At K = 1 the counted traffic is about 568 GB/s, above the
  554 GB/s Triad ceiling: the best read/write kernel for a one-load-one-store pattern is not
  Triad, so that exception is a property of the ceiling, as A4's first reading suggested.
* **At M = 256 the model is off the other way.** BF16 moves about 1.4× the modeled bytes
  (read share 0.87: activations, outputs and in-call repacking that the weights-only model
  leaves out), so its counted intensity is 168 against 237 modeled; INT8 moves 0.82×.
  Prefill points on the roofline shift left when counted bytes are used.
