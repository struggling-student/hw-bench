# Scientific claims and evidence matrix

**Validated** means the archived measurements, controls and scope support the stated conclusion. **Qualified** means the observed result is defensible with the stated limitation. **Unsupported** flags a causal or performance claim that exceeds the evidence. **Redundant** marks measurements retained for context. **Missing** denotes an unmeasured quantity. Confidence applies to the statement as scoped. The [figure and table selection](THESIS_FIGURES_AND_TABLES.md) defines F1–F6 and T1–T3. The [selection policy](../configs/analysis.json) lists the exact primary, extended and uncore campaigns.

| Question and hypothesis | Experiment and numerical evidence | Classification and defense |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the platform expose local tiers and the expected compute resources? | The platform and topology records of `flat-ustreams-20261019T205225Z` and `cache-ucapacity-20261031T192305Z` show 2 × 56 physical cores at one thread/core, 64 GiB HBM and eight 64 GiB DDR DIMMs per socket. Cache HMAT records 64 GiB, 64-byte direct-mapped write-back. | **Validated**, high. Observed topology and firmware cache description are ready. DDR5-4800 is a SKU reference. HBM-only/SNC4 performance on these hosts is outside the measured scope. |
| Does local HBM sustain more bandwidth, and where does core scaling flatten? | `flat-bandwidth2-20261011T230637Z`, October primary/xcheck and repeats, `flat-ustreams-20261019T205225Z` and `flat-usweep-20261020T210357Z`. Useful read about 387→542 GB/s HBM and 268→262 DDR from 28→56 cores. Counted references 553 and 261 GB/s. F1, T1. | **Validated**, high. Quote same-kernel, same-scope ratios. Separate useful rates and CAS references. November launch medians on both sockets extend the observed range. |
| Is the HBM plateau associated with package clipping and reduced uncore clock? | U1, E6 `flat-streamgap_e6-20261019T212743Z`, E7c `flat-streamgap_e7c-20261101T103151Z`. U1 350 W, 100% PCU power clipping, zero thermal clipping, 1.54 GHz uncore at 56 cores. Initial window 377 W, +13% CAS, +11% uncore. E6 531–532 vs 579–583 GB/s with 1.53 vs 1.64–1.66 GHz. F2. | **Qualified**, high for clipping and covariation, moderate for the broader mechanism. Power limiting and throughput change are observed. Exact recovery at a counterfactual PL1 and the component power split are **unsupported**. |
| Can a comparable STREAM convention account for much of the 554 vs 700 GB/s Triad gap? | E1–E5 `flat-streamgap-20261016T132035Z`, `flat-streamgap_e4-20261016T132755Z`, `flat-streamgap_e5-20261016T135914Z`. E4 559→594→673 GB/s with unroll and data changes, and 733 GB/s best. Vendored STREAM 678–711 GB/s for selected builds. E7/E7c report 641 useful and 621 CAS GB/s for constant Triad in the counted repeat. | **Validated**, high for the benchmark-protocol comparison. Useful bandwidth, traffic mix and elapsed window matter. **Qualified** for cross-system attribution because the exact published build, data and platform state are incomplete. E1–E4 tuning variants are **redundant** as principal figures. |
| Does HBM lower dependent-load latency? | `flat-hierarchy-20261015T201637Z`, `flat-loadedlat-20261016T124007Z`, `flat-uloaded-20261019T211812Z`. Idle 139 ns HBM vs 116 ns DDR in the paired test. At 55 streaming cores, 209 ns at 549 GB/s vs 373 ns at 262 GB/s. T1. | **Validated**, high for these chase/load conditions. Little's-law concurrency is a derived estimate from a different requester than McCalpin's measured occupancy. |
| Can independent sockets preserve locality and aggregate their bandwidth? | `flat-span-20261012T073217Z`, paired dual selection and `flat-bandwidth-search-20261101T112108Z`. Dual rate 0.984–1.017× isolated sum across 29 cases. October spanning HBM read 1,100 vs 265 GB/s with local first-touch vs interleave. November 1,159 vs 254 GB/s. F4. | **Validated**, high for independent workers and the tested spanning process. Independent scaling does not predict communication-intensive inference. |
| Is the remote HBM penalty real, and what traffic accompanies it? | `flat-remotehbm-20261012T215047Z` and `flat-uremote-20261019T205814Z`. 46.5 GB/s vs 539 GB/s local in the 56-thread repeat. Counted run shows about twice the useful HBM reads and UPI data flits per useful line. F4. | **Validated**, high for the penalty and excess measured traffic. **Qualified** for mechanism. The origin of repeat transactions is **missing**. |
| Is Cache's nominal HBM capacity a residency threshold? | `cache-ucapacity-20261031T192305Z`, `cache-ucacherep-20261031T192313Z`, `cache-ucacherep-20261031T192317Z`, `cache-ucacherep-20261031T192321Z`, and `cache-ucapacity-20261020T201654Z`. Approximately 92%, 45%, 12% hits at 8, 64, 128 GiB. Earlier 56 GiB read 531 versus later 149–153 GB/s. F3. | **Validated**, high for the observed curve and variance. **Qualified** for the cause of the earlier high rate. Conflict misses are plausible, and older comparable hit evidence is **missing**. |
| Do misses contribute to Cache latency and spilled GEMM behavior? | `cache-ucachelat-20261031T192333Z`, `flat-flatfar-20261016T121759Z`, `cache-ugemm-20261031T192309Z` and counted capacity run. Chase about 165→227 ns, estimated hits 84→11% over 16–128 GiB. Flat DDR control grows 4 ns. At 96 GiB kernels with similar hit fraction reach different DDR fill rates. | **Qualified**, high for measured trends, moderate for attribution. The Flat control makes translation alone implausible. CPU PMUs do not localize every miss or bottleneck. |
| Can the native paths approach their per-cycle rates? | `flat-isa-20261011T225342Z`, `flat-chains-20261012T205216Z`, `flat-ustreams-20261019T205225Z` and Flat repeats. 94–99% at measured clocks. AMX BF16 112.95 TFLOP/s and INT8 225.92 T integer op/s near 1.99 GHz. T2. | **Validated**, high for register/tile throughput. **Qualified** for the exact 32 op/core/cycle AVX-512 BF16 reference inferred from the observed chain plateau and independent instruction data on other cores. |
| Do controller-based Rooflines pass a scope- and byte-matched audit? | `flat-ugemmconv-20261020T201733Z`, `flat-uatlas-20261020T204045Z`, `flat-usweep-20261020T210357Z`, `cache-uatlas-20261031T192329Z` and the October 31 Cache streams. Cache 517 GB/s read at 16 GiB, 175 GB/s Triad at 96 GiB. No principal Flat point or matched Cache atlas point exceeds its reference. F5/F6, T3. | **Validated** as an empirical consistency check. **Qualified** as a limiting-regime model. October modeled exceptions are preserved. Cache lookup-byte and Flat controller-byte intensity have different physical scope. |
| Does HBM help decode-like projection and does the gain shrink with reuse? | `flat-ugemmconv-20261020T201733Z` and `flat-gemmvalid-20261012T211909Z` pool/overhead/layout controls. M = 1 HBM/DDR 1.43× BF16 and 1.51× INT8. M = 4096 1.07× and 1.004×. M = 4096 BF16 HBM measured 486 versus modeled 1,792 FLOP/B. F5/F6, T3. | **Validated**, high for synthetic projection rates. **Qualified** for prefill bottleneck and weight-reread attribution. Full-model throughput and real-weight stream sensitivity are **missing** and outside the measured claim. |
| Does higher local bandwidth reduce energy per streamed byte or projection operation? | `flat-ustreams-20261019T205225Z`, `flat-uidle-20261031T200539Z`, `cache-uidle-20261031T200541Z`, `flat-udual-20261031T182202Z` and `cache-udual-20261031T192325Z`. Full-rate socket read 679 vs 1,625 pJ/B HBM/DDR. Gross node idle 310–315 W RAPL, about 400 W BMC. T3 includes projection pJ/op. | **Validated**, high for the RAPL scopes and tested loads. **Qualified** for wall power given slow BMC sampling. Isolated transfer energy and measured LLM energy/token are **unsupported**. |

For exact workload and reference campaign IDs on each Roofline row, use the [point audit](../data/processed/thesis/roofline_reference_audit.csv). Tables use separately aggregated launch statistics. The audit uses the median useful throughput divided by the median per-window intensity, so its implied rate can differ slightly from the median per-window bandwidth.
